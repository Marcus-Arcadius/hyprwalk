**MAToggles:**
- `item`: {id(MenuItem): (param, value)}, from its emulation of MA's ParameterAssignerPass.
- `declared`: parameter → (value type, default).
- `controls()`: the virtual menu, as [(menu path tuple, control dict)].
- `rules`: {('a', go) | ('s', smr, shape): [(value, conditions, inverted)]}.
- `apply(params, vals)`: the last rule in effect wins, and parent activeness is resolved to a fixed point.
- `merged`: [(priority, order, Controller, param view, replaces FX)].

**Analysis:**
- `menu` and `eparams` include MA's additions.
- `evaluate()` runs FX plus the merged controllers, then `mat.apply`.
- There's a fast path identical to the old code when there's no MA.
- `toggles()`, `gestures()`, `chained()` (post-MA children) and `dynamics()` (skips dead PBs) live here.

**ModularAvatar:**
- `parent`, `kids` and `nm` are the post-MA tree.
- Other state: `deleted`, `retarget` {id: dest GO}, `dead` PBs, `blocks`, `vparent`, `U0`, and `db` (the bone database).
- Methods: `set_parent`, `find`, `objref`, `merge_armatures`/`rmerge`/`prune_physbones`/`retarget_meshes`, `bone_proxies`, `move_tos`, `blockers`, `up()`/`down()`/`ignores()`.
- `apply(js, binc)` rewrites the exported GLB:
  - It moves nodes, keeping their worlds, when the post-MA anchor differs or their GLB parent is deleted.
  - It retargets skin joints with `IBM' = W(dest)⁻¹ · FLIP·V·U⁻¹·FLIP · W(src) · IBM` and appends a new MAT4 accessor.
  - It deletes the merged bones' nodes and reindexes.

**convert():**
- Order: Avatar → adopt → Analysis → humanoid → OutfitSetup → Build.import_models → compute → OutfitSetup.fix (A-pose) → ModularAvatar(av, human, U, specs) → Jaw check → toggles → arrange → place → materials → shapes → names → export → patch_materials → `ma.apply` → Settings.
- `--outfit` prefabs are put under the avatar root by `put_on()` before Avatar() is built.

**Conventions:**
- U is each GameObject's Unity world matrix, with the avatar at the origin.
- A Unity point p has glTF world position `FLIP @ U @ p`.
- Every FBX becomes its own Blender armature, with one skin per armature and every bone a joint.
- The plugin skins with `restGlobal[joint]·IBM` and ignores the mesh node's transform and `skin.skeleton`. Duplicate joints are fine.
- Blender's arrange and place use the original hierarchy with the final U. `ModularAvatar.apply()` fixes up the GLB afterwards.
