## Lip sync: what the plugin heard

I said each vowel when the notification asked. check.sh reads `hyprctl hypr3d avatar lipsync` every 0.12 s for 3 s per prompt, and reports medians:

| prompt | run 1 | run 2 |
|---|---|---|
| a | -177 dB | -177 |
| i | -57 (ee 0.01) | -177 |
| u | -45 (aa 0.05, ee 0.01) | -177 |
| e | -57 | -177 |
| o | -56 | -177 |
| sss | -56 | -177 |
| silence | -59 | -177 |

**-177.0 dB is exact digital silence.**
- The level is 20·log10(rms + 1e-9) + 3.01 ([src/lipsync.cpp:139](src/lipsync.cpp#L139)).
- It's only computed when a whole window of samples has arrived (`feed()` calls `window()`). If nothing arrives, it stays at reset's -120.
- So the stream got buffers of zeros: in all of run 2 (123 readings, about 25 s) and for most of run 1's first prompt.
- The plugin said "listening" the whole time. "listening" only means the PipeWire thread runs (`CMicrophone::on()`, [src/mic.cpp:126](src/mic.cpp#L126)).

**In run 1, my voice arrived far too quietly for the thresholds:**
- The readings were -45 to -57 dB (medians), over a room at -59 dB.
- The mapping is fixed:
  - `gate = -52` and `full = -20` dBFS ([src/lipsync.hpp:54](src/lipsync.hpp#L54)), used at [src/lipsync.cpp:140](src/lipsync.cpp#L140) and [:282](src/lipsync.cpp#L282)
  - a window must also be 10 dB over the noise floor ([:259](src/lipsync.cpp#L259))
- At my levels, the mouth opens 0–20%, which is what I saw.
- The medians may undercount: I may not have kept each vowel going for the whole 3 s.

**The synthetic tests hide this:**
- vowels.py's voices peak at 0.5, about -12 dBFS RMS.
- `quiet_a` is 40 dB down ([tools/test/synth/vowels.py:123](tools/test/synth/vowels.py#L123)), about -52 dBFS. It must keep the mouth shut: that's lipsync_check.sh's "whisper-quiet voice".
- My real voice through my real mic is about as loud as that "whisper".

**Why zeros is still unknown.** The candidates:
- (a) The Yeti X muted on the device itself: pressing its knob mutes it, and its LEDs turn red. A muted one may send exact zeros.
- (b) The Yeti X sending zeros for a while after PipeWire resumes it from suspend. That would explain run 1's first prompt, but not all of run 2.
- (c) The stream isn't linked to anything, but gets scheduled anyway with zero-filled buffers. Find out whether an unlinked pw_stream with AUTOCONNECT gets `process()` calls.
- (d) WirePlumber linked it to another source.
- Not the cause: WirePlumber's `~/.local/state/wireplumber/stream-properties` has `Input/Audio:application.name:hypr3d` at volume 1, not muted, so this isn't a restored stream mute.
- The stream itself is set up at [src/mic.cpp:82-102](src/mic.cpp#L82): `PW_ID_ANY` with AUTOCONNECT, F32 mono at 48 kHz, media.role "Communication".
